  <h1 id="balsam-title" class="balsam-title">Himalayan Balsam Removal</h1>

  <div class="balsam-card" role="region" aria-label="Himalayan Balsam event details">
    <p>GEMS is a student-led group promoting sustainability across campus and beyond. We run seasonal projects from September through April/May aiming to achieve Green Impact Awards annually. New members from any department are welcome throughout the year.</p>

    <h2>About the project</h2>
    <p>Himalayan balsam (Impatiens glandulifera) is an invasive plant that spreads rapidly along waterways and suppresses native species. Our removal project organises volunteer days to pull balsam before it seeds, helping restore riverside habitats and supporting local biodiversity.</p>

    <h2>Events</h2>
    <p>We hold weekly meetings on Wednesdays from 1–2pm to discuss project updates and proposals. Daryl Stump (Dept. of Archaeology & Dept. of Environment and Geography) provides oversight, while the group remains primarily student-led. Volunteer clearance and health & safety briefings are provided before field sessions.</p>

    <h2>Volunteer days</h2>
    <p>Volunteer days are run periodically during the growing season. Tasks typically involve supervised pulling of plants, safe disposal, and site restoration. Equipment and guidance are provided — just bring suitable outdoor clothing and a willingness to help.</p>

    <h2>Contact</h2>
    <p>If you sign up to become a member you will be added to our Google Chat, which is our main form of communication. For queries, email <a href="mailto:gems@example.com">gems@example.com</a> or visit our <a href="/events.html">events</a> page for upcoming sessions.</p>
